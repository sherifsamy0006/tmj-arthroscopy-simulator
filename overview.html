Our road map

We are here: resting anatomy and compartment boundaries. Textures and hydraulic distension have not started.

  1. Available · one source model and review tools. Shared bone/disc coordinates, corrected contact geometry, layer isolation and cutaways. Specimen imaging validation remains open.
  2. NOW · finish anatomy and boundaries. Test sampling candidates with conforming joins (C04: 11 full cells + one partial cell recovered), then review remaining computational closures and the extreme medial fascial transition; distinguish synovial recesses from computational closures; verify the whole inferior compartment and obtain anatomical review.
  3. NEXT · appearance and scope view. Add tissue materials, lighting and a reproducible arthroscope view after the anatomy gate. Appearance must preserve the reviewed boundaries.
  4. LATER · fluid distension and equipment sequence. Model saline delivery, inflow/outflow and deforming tissue boundaries, followed by the device/setup steps. Pressure–volume behavior and compartment-specific limits need evidence; no uniform scale-up.
  5. LATER · portals and instruments. Constrained scope/cannula motion, insertion sequence, collisions and anatomical hazards.
  6. LATER · training and validation. Landmark recall, one repeatable task, defined error metrics, surgeon review and phone/performance testing before skill claims.
  7. FINAL · linked MRI / CT navigation. Scope-tip position and direction on registered images and 3D of the same joint; verify registration and jaw configuration.

Later stage · hydraulic distension, scope and imaging

Arthroscopy generally creates working space using fluid distension and controlled outflow. Conventional access is usually to the superior compartment; excessive superior distension can compress the inferior compartment. The later simulator must show jaw position, cannula/scope, inflow and outflow, pressure-dependent local unfolding and leakage. This resting model implements none of those mechanics. Inferior arthroscopy and compartment coupling · Irrigation pressure and flow study.

Scope-tip position and direction on MRI are deferred until anatomy is reviewed. They require images and geometry of the same joint, a verified registration and documented jaw pose.
